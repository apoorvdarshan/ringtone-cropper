Make it ring.

  1. Download your ringtone

    Choose Android and save your clip as an MP3.

  2. Open your audio file

    In Files by Google, open Audio and play your downloaded clip.

  3. Make it your ringtone

    Tap More → Set as ringtone. Allow settings access if prompted.

Settings vary by phone. You can also look for “Add ringtone” in your sound settings. Google’s guide ↗

  1. Save your 30-second clip

    Choose iPhone and download the M4A to Files. M4R is also available for computer workflows.

  2. Open it in GarageBand

    Open Audio Recorder → Tracks → Loops → Files. Set the song section to Automatic, then drag in your clip.

  3. Set your new ringtone

    In My Songs, share the project as a Ringtone, export, then choose “Use sound as”.

GarageBand shortens clips longer than 30 seconds. Apple’s guide ↗
