Add it to your iPhone

  1. Save the ringtone as .m4r.
  2. Connect your iPhone with a cable and select it in the Finder sidebar.
  3. Drag the .m4r file onto the iPhone window. It copies over straight away.
  4. On your iPhone, open Settings › Sounds & Haptics › Ringtone and tap it.
  1. Save it as .m4a. Safari puts it in the Files app, under Downloads.
  2. Open GarageBand, start an Audio Recorder project and switch to the tracks view.
  3. Tap the loop icon, choose Files, and drag your .m4a onto the timeline.
  4. In My Songs, touch and hold the project, then tap Share › Ringtone › Export.
  5. Tap “Use sound as…” and choose Standard Ringtone.
  1. Save the ringtone as .m4r.
  2. Open the Apple Devices app (or iTunes) and connect your iPhone.
  3. Drag the .m4r onto your iPhone in the sidebar.
  4. On your iPhone, open Settings › Sounds & Haptics › Ringtone and tap it.

Ringtones can be up to 40 seconds. Text tones and alerts stop at 30, so 29.9 s works for both.

Runs in your browser with ffmpeg.wasm. Only use audio you have the rights to.
