How to put the ringtone on your iPhone

💻 From a Mac (easiest)

  1. Download the .m4r.
  2. Connect your iPhone and open it in Finder.
  3. Drag the .m4r onto the iPhone window (General tab).
  4. iPhone → Settings → Sounds & Haptics → Ringtone.

📱 On the iPhone only

  1. Download the .m4a or .mp3 — it saves to Files.
  2. Open GarageBand → new Audio Recorder → loop icon → Files → drag the song in.
  3. In My Songs, long-press the project → Share → Ringtone → Export.
  4. Tap “Use sound as…” → Standard Ringtone.

🪟 From Windows

  1. Download the .m4r.
  2. Open the Apple Devices app (or iTunes) with your iPhone connected.
  3. Drag the .m4r into the device's Tones section.
  4. Pick it in Settings → Sounds & Haptics.

iPhone ringtones can be up to 40 seconds; alert/text tones up to 30. 29.9s is the safe default.
