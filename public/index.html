Get it on your iPhone

💻

From a Mac

  1. Download the .m4r.
  2. Connect your iPhone, open it in Finder.
  3. Drag the file onto the iPhone window.
  4. Settings → Sounds & Haptics → Ringtone.
📱

On iPhone only

  1. Download .m4a or .mp3 to Files.
  2. Import it into GarageBand.
  3. My Songs → long-press → Share → Ringtone.
  4. Use sound as Standard Ringtone.
🪟

From Windows

  1. Download the .m4r.
  2. Open Apple Devices (or iTunes).
  3. Drag it into the device's Tones.
  4. Pick it in Sounds & Haptics.

Ringtones can be up to 40s; text & alert tones up to 30s. 29.9s is the safe default.
